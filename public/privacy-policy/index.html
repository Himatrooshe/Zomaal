Zomaal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Zomaal operates the Zomaal service. For privacy questions and requests concerning your information, contact guighirfb2@gmail.com.

About this policy

Zomaal provides tools for merchants to manage stores, orders, customers, inventory, shipping, staff, expenses, advertising reports and business messaging. This policy explains the information processed through these services. The information involved depends on the features and integrations you use.

Merchants supply customer and staff information to run their businesses. If you are a merchant's customer, contact that merchant about your order and its handling of your information. You may also contact Zomaal about information processed through our service.

Information we process and why

  • Account and store information: names, phone numbers, business and contact details, login credentials and permissions support registration, authentication, store administration and access control.
  • Customer and transaction information: customer names, phone numbers, addresses, order and shipment details support order handling, customer service, delivery and returns.
  • Business records: product, inventory, purchase, expense, staff, subscription and invoice records support the management and reporting features requested by merchants.
  • Connected accounts: authorized account identifiers, integration credentials, campaign information and advertising metrics support connections and reporting requested by users.
  • WhatsApp Business information: business-account and phone-number identifiers, approved templates, recipient numbers, message variables and delivery-status records support configured business messages and their reporting. This information comes from merchants and their authorized WhatsApp Business connections.
  • Operational information: request, error and service logs support reliability, troubleshooting and protection against misuse.

How information is shared

Store information is available to authorized store users according to their permissions. We use hosting, storage and other service providers to operate Zomaal, including Google Cloud infrastructure.

When you enable an integration, relevant information is exchanged with that provider to perform the requested function. Depending on your configuration, providers include Meta for WhatsApp and advertising, Snapchat and Google for advertising, ecommerce platforms, couriers, and payment or authentication providers. For example, a WhatsApp delivery update shares the recipient's phone number and message content with Meta. Providers also process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or in response to lawful requests, or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

Storage, retention and security

We use authentication, store-based permissions and encryption of stored integration credentials to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Our infrastructure and service providers may process information outside your country.

We retain information for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service and meeting applicable operational, security and legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of information and those purposes. Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove copies from backups or records that must be retained for applicable obligations.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can manage available account details and integrations through Zomaal. Disconnecting an integration stops its use through that connection; it does not automatically delete all previously stored records or information held independently by the provider.

You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing guighirfb2@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the account or store and describe your request. Do not include passwords, access tokens or payment-card details. We may verify your identity and authority, or coordinate with the relevant merchant, before acting on a request.

Zomaal also provides an account-deletion request function with a 30-day cancellation period. Deleting a merchant owner account affects its stores and associated records. See our data-deletion instructions for the contact process and available account controls. Any retention required for applicable obligations is considered when handling a request.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Contact guighirfb2@gmail.com with questions about this policy or your information.
