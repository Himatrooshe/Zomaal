Zomaal Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: 31 October 2026

Request deletion by email

  1. Email guighirfb2@gmail.com with the subject Zomaal data deletion request.
  2. Identify your Zomaal account or store and describe what you want deleted. For a connected Meta or WhatsApp Business account, identify the relevant business account or business number if known.
  3. Do not include passwords, access tokens, app secrets or card details. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and authority over the account.

You can use this process even if you cannot sign in. Requests are handled subject to identity verification, the merchant's responsibilities and applicable retention obligations. We will respond using the contact details supplied with your request.

Account deletion and connected services

Zomaal's account-deletion function schedules deletion with a 30-day cancellation period. Deleting an owner account also affects its stores and associated records. If you cannot find or use the account-deletion control, use the email process above.

Disconnecting Facebook, WhatsApp or another integration is separate from requesting deletion of records already stored in Zomaal. Specify in your request whether you want integration-related records or the entire account deleted. Information independently held by Meta or another provider is subject to that provider's deletion process.

If you are a merchant's customer, include the merchant's store name and enough information to locate your records. We may coordinate your request with that merchant.
